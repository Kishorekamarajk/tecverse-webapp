FAQ

Sponsor FAQ

Quick answers for brands and organisations planning to partner with Tec-Verse 2026.

Which sponsor packages are available? +

Tec-Verse sponsorship is available through five official packages: Platinum, Gold, Silver, Copper and Bronze. Each package includes different levels of branding, passes, session access, stall visibility and hospitality benefits.

Why is email OTP verification required? +

OTP verification confirms that the official email address belongs to the organisation or authorised contact person. This helps the team respond to genuine sponsorship enquiries faster.

Do we need to pay on this website? +

No bank, card or payment details are collected through this public form. After submission, the Tec-Verse team will contact you for package confirmation, invoice details and payment follow-up.

What branding benefits do sponsors receive? +

Branding benefits can include logo placement on marketing materials, event sign boards, kits, main-stage branding, technical write-up in the event report and other visibility benefits based on the selected package.

Is stall space included in sponsorship? +

Yes, sponsor packages include stall visibility according to the tier. Platinum includes a premium category stall, Gold includes a large stall, Silver includes a medium stall and Copper includes a small stall.

How many event passes are included? +

Platinum, Gold, Silver and Copper packages list 10 technical session passes. Hospitality access such as VIP lounge and dining varies by package tier.

Can we request package clarification or changes? +

Please select the closest official package and mention your requirement in the message field. The sponsorship team will review it and contact you for clarification.

What happens after form submission? +

After your enquiry is submitted, the Tec-Verse team reviews the organisation details, preferred package and message. They will then contact you for confirmation, documentation and next steps.
