About Tec-verse

Shaping Tomorrow Through Technology and Innovation

Tec-verse is a technology exhibition and conclave shaped by MeitY's premier R&D organisations: C-DAC, SAMEER, and C-MET. Its first edition, Tec-verse 2025, was held on 27-28 June 2025 at Manekshaw Centre, New Delhi, as a unified platform to showcase and commercialise indigenous deep-tech solutions.

The 2025 edition brought together more than 150 technologies from domains including high-performance computing, quantum technologies, AI, cybersecurity, semiconductors, telecom, healthcare, agriculture, power electronics, robotics, UAVs, IoT, and Industry 4.0. It connected ministries, industry, startups, academia, and research communities around market-ready Indian technology.

Journey of Tec-verse

From the first MeitY R&D showcase in 2025 to the next platform for Indian deep-tech collaboration.

TEC 2025

First Edition in New Delhi

Tec-verse 2025 was launched as a two-day MeitY event jointly organised by C-DAC, SAMEER, and C-MET. It showcased 150+ cutting-edge technologies, welcomed 1000+ participants, and recorded product launches, technology transfers, and MoUs with industry.

TEC 2026

Expanding the National Platform

Tec-verse 2026 builds on the 2025 foundation, continuing the mission to connect MeitY R&D, industry, startups, academia, and government stakeholders around indigenous technologies with real-world impact.
