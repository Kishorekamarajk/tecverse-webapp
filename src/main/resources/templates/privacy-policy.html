Privacy Policy

Tec-Verse 2026 website and mobile application

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Tec-Verse 2026 is organised by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC). This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be collected, used, stored, shared and otherwise processed when you visit https://tec-verse.cdac.in or use the Tec-Verse 2026 Android mobile application (package name: in.cdac.tecverse.tec_app).

This policy applies specifically to the Tec-Verse 2026 Android application and the Tec-Verse event website. It identifies the information processed by the app, why it is processed, who receives it, how it is protected and retained, and how you can request deletion. C-DAC is responsible for the app and its event services.

Important: Please do not submit information that is not requested or needed for your registration or enquiry. Where consent is required by applicable law, it will be requested through an appropriate mechanism.

1. Personal Data We Collect

The app uses information submitted during Tec-Verse registration and information created when you use app features. Information is sent to Tec-Verse servers over HTTPS to provide account and event services.

1.1 Registration and account information

Registration and account information may include:

  • Full name;
  • Email address and mobile number;
  • Password, stored as a one-way hash rather than as plain text;
  • Registration category;
  • Organisation, company or educational institution;
  • Designation and city;
  • Student identification number, where applicable; and
  • Preferred attendance days.

The app uses these details to identify your registration, sign you in, display your profile and digital pass, support account recovery, and send event-related communications. Passwords are stored by the service as one-way hashes; the app does not store your password in plain text.

1.2 Passport information

Foreign nationals may be asked to provide passport details, such as passport number, expiry date and name as shown on the passport, where these details are required for event entry, identity verification or security procedures. Passport information should be accessible only to authorised personnel who need it for the relevant purpose.

1.3 Exhibitor and sponsor enquiries

If you contact us about exhibiting or sponsoring, we may collect the contact and company information you submit, together with documents or other supporting information you choose to upload. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and coordinate potential or confirmed participation.

1.4 Mobile application and feature usage

When you use app features, we may process information such as:

  • Technology interests that you select;
  • Text questions submitted to the in-app event FAQ search, including text recognised from voice input;
  • Event-related notifications delivered or displayed through the app; and
  • Registration and event check-in status.

This information is used to provide the requested app features and support event operations.

1.5 Microphone and voice search

Voice search is optional. Before Android's microphone permission prompt appears, the app displays an explanation and lets you choose whether to continue. Android asks for microphone access only after you tap the microphone and agree to continue; the app does not turn on microphone access automatically at installation. The speech-recognition service available on your device processes your speech and returns recognized text. The app sends that text to the Tec-Verse FAQ service to answer your query; Tec-Verse does not upload or store raw audio on its servers. Your device or speech provider may process audio under its own privacy policy. You can deny or later revoke microphone permission and type your question instead.

1.6 Device permissions, location and maps

The app requests internet access to connect to Tec-Verse services. It requests microphone access only for voice search. It does not request GPS, contacts, camera, photos or media permissions. If you enter a starting point and tap directions, that location is included in the directions link opened in Google Maps or Apple Maps; the selected maps provider then processes it under its own privacy policy. The app does not track your location in the background. The app does not contain advertising or use personal data for targeted advertising.

1.7 Technical and security information

When the app connects to the service, the server and network infrastructure process connection data such as IP address, request time and requested endpoint to deliver the response, maintain security and diagnose service problems. Authentication tokens are stored on the device using the operating system's secure storage. The app does not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs.

1.8 App fonts

The app uses the Google Fonts package for typography. If the required font files are not already available on the device, the package may request them from Google Fonts. Google may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and the requested font. Font delivery and any related processing are governed by Google's terms and privacy information.

2. How We Use Personal Data

Subject to applicable law, personal data may be used for the following purposes:

  • Registration and account management: to create, maintain and administer your event registration and account.
  • Authentication and recovery: to verify email addresses using one-time passwords (OTPs), authenticate accounts and facilitate password resets.
  • Digital passes: to issue and manage digital event passes and associated QR codes.
  • Entry and attendance: to verify registration, facilitate entry at the venue and record check-in where applicable.
  • Event communications: to send registration confirmations, schedule information, operational notices and other messages related to Tec-Verse 2026.
  • Event planning: to understand attendance requirements, coordinate event activities and support venue operations.
  • Enquiry management: to respond to exhibitor and sponsor enquiries and coordinate participation.
  • Application features: to provide event FAQ search, voice search and other features you choose to use.
  • Security and compliance: to protect the website, application, participants and event operations, and to comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not use personal data for targeted advertising.

3. Digital Passes, QR Codes and Event Check-In

Registered participants may receive a digital pass with a QR code to support registration verification and event entry. When an authorised event staff member scans a QR code, the system may retrieve the registration information needed to verify the registration and facilitate check-in. The system may record check-in status for event administration and attendance reporting.

Access to registration and check-in information should be limited to personnel authorised for event operations or security. QR codes are intended for event-related purposes, not advertising. Please avoid sharing your digital pass or QR code publicly because another person could attempt to use it without your permission.

4. How We Share Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may allow access to or disclose relevant information where necessary to operate the event services, respond to your requests, protect event operations or meet applicable legal requirements.

4.1 Authorised C-DAC personnel

Authorised C-DAC personnel and event staff may access information relevant to their responsibilities, such as registration administration, participant communication, attendee verification and event security.

4.2 Service providers

The following service providers receive information when you use the related feature:

  • Website hosting, infrastructure and database providers;
  • Email and SMS providers used to deliver OTPs, confirmations and event communications;
  • Google Fonts, which may receive connection information and font requests when the app retrieves font files;
  • Your device's speech-recognition provider, which processes audio when you choose voice search; and
  • Google Maps or Apple Maps, which receives the destination and any starting point you enter when you choose to open directions.

Hosting, database, email and SMS providers process registration and account information as needed to operate the Tec-Verse service and deliver one-time passwords, confirmations and event communications. These providers process information to provide services to C-DAC and are subject to their applicable privacy terms and service arrangements. We do not sell personal data.

4.3 Legal, regulatory and security disclosures

We may disclose relevant information where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from an authorised government or security authority. We will handle such disclosures in accordance with applicable requirements.

4.4 No sale or targeted advertising

We do not sell personal data or disclose it for third-party targeted advertising.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where needed to support functions such as session management, security, navigation or service reliability. The cookies and technologies actually used depend on the website's configuration.

If analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies are used, their purposes and applicable choices should be explained through the relevant website notice or settings. We do not use personal data for targeted advertising.

6. Data Security and Retention

6.1 Security measures

We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction. Measures used by the relevant systems may include:

  • HTTPS encryption for data transmitted between supported clients and services;
  • One-way password hashing rather than storing passwords in plain text;
  • Secure storage of application session information on the device; and
  • Access restrictions for administrative systems and authorised personnel.

These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee complete security. A person's data could still be exposed through vulnerabilities, compromised accounts or devices, or a service provider incident. Apps normally run within Android's application sandbox, but this does not make data immune to malware, device compromise, vulnerabilities or account misuse. Users should protect account credentials and avoid sharing passwords or digital passes with unauthorised persons.

6.2 Retention of personal data

We keep registration and account data while it is needed to administer the event, provide account features, meet reporting requirements, resolve disputes and comply with law. Temporary verification codes expire and are removed after use or expiry. Device session tokens are removed when you sign out, your session expires, or you delete your account.

When you delete your account in the app after confirming with your current password, the service removes the registration and the linked app preferences, notifications and check-in records from active service records. Deleting or uninstalling the app alone does not delete your server-side account. If you cannot sign in, you can request deletion through the public account deletion page. We retain information only where required by law or for a specific legitimate security, audit or event-administration obligation, and limit that information to the relevant purpose.

7. Your Choices, Requests and Account Deletion

7.1 Access and correction

You can view the registration details made available through your account in the app. If information is inaccurate or you need help with your personal data, contact us using the details in Section 12. Requests will be handled under applicable law and the procedures available for Tec-Verse 2026.

7.2 Account deletion

You can submit an account deletion request through either of the following methods:

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete Account and follow the instructions.
  • On the website: visit the Tec-Verse account deletion page and follow the instructions provided there.

Account deletion removes the registration and associated digital pass, app preferences, notifications and check-in data from active service records. The in-app deletion flow asks you to confirm with your current password. Deletion also ends access to the account and its app features.

Certain records may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law or necessary for legitimate security, audit or event-administration purposes. Any such retention should be limited to the relevant purpose and period.

7.3 Other privacy requests and identity verification

You may contact us with questions about how your personal data is handled or to submit requests available to you under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. Requests will be handled through the applicable C-DAC procedures and within legally prescribed timelines.

8. Children's Privacy

The event and app are intended for professionals, students and visitors aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Where individuals under 18 may register or use the services, applicable legal requirements concerning children's personal data must be followed. Where parental or guardian consent or additional safeguards are required by law, the appropriate procedures should be used. If you believe a child's personal data has been collected inappropriately, contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

Some features may rely on third-party services, such as a device's speech-recognition service or a maps provider that you choose to open. When you use those features, the relevant information may be processed by the third party under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

If the website or app links to an external website or service, review that provider's privacy information before submitting information to it.

10. Processing and Storage Locations

Personal data may be processed or stored in the locations used by C-DAC and its relevant service providers. Where processing involves a transfer of personal data across borders, the transfer should be conducted in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant safeguards.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Tec-Verse website, mobile application, event operations, data-processing practices or applicable legal requirements. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated. Where required, we will provide appropriate notice of material changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
