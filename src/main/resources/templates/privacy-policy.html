Privacy Policy

Tec-Verse 2026 website and mobile app · Last updated: 28 October 2026

Tec-Verse 2026 (Android package: in.cdac.tecverse.tec_app) is organised by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC). This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Tec-Verse website (https://tec-verse.cdac.in) and the Tec-Verse 2026 Android app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Registration details: name, email address, mobile number, password (stored only as a one-way hash), registration category, organisation / institution, designation, city, student ID (students only) and preferred attendance days.
  • Passport details (foreign nationals only): passport number, expiry date and name as shown on the passport, used for event security and entry.
  • Exhibitor and sponsor enquiries: company and contact details, and documents you upload.
  • App usage: technology interests you select in the app, notifications sent to you, and your event check-in status.
  • Microphone (optional): if you use voice search in the app, your speech is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service. We do not record or store audio.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To register you for the event, issue your digital pass and QR code, and verify entry at the venue.
  • To verify your email with a one-time password (OTP) and to let you reset your password.
  • To send event updates, confirmations and notifications related to Tec-Verse 2026.
  • To plan and secure the event (attendance numbers, venue access).

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It is accessible only to authorised C-DAC event staff and the service providers that host our servers and send our emails, who process it on our behalf. We may disclose data where required by law or by government security authorities for event security.

4. Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored as hashes, app sessions are stored in the device's secure storage, and access to the admin system is restricted. We keep registration data only as long as needed for Tec-Verse 2026 and related reporting, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Your choices and account deletion

You can view your details in the app, and you can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account permanently removes your registration, digital pass, preferences and notifications.

6. Children

The event and app are intended for professionals, students and visitors aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. For any privacy question or request, contact us at tec-verse@cdac.in.
