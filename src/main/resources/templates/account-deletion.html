Delete your Tec-Verse account

Tec-Verse 2026 app by C-DAC

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Tec-Verse 2026 app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, send an email from your registered email address to tec-verse@cdac.in with the subject “Account deletion request” and your registration reference number (if you have it). We verify the request and delete the account within 7 working days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your registration: name, email, mobile number, password, category, organisation and passport details.
  • Your digital event pass and QR code, check-in record, app preferences and notifications.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep a record; any such record is kept only for the period the law requires. See our Privacy Policy for details.
