Privacy Policy

Application: Tec-Verse 2026 (Android package: in.cdac.tecverse.tec_app)
Official Website: https://tec-verse.cdac.in
Developer & Organization: Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)
Last Updated: 28 October 2026

Tec-Verse 2026 is organized by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), an autonomous scientific society under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. This Privacy Policy discloses our policies regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of personal data collected through the Tec-Verse 2026 Android mobile application (in.cdac.tecverse.tec_app) and the official event website.

1. Data We Collect

  • Registration & Delegate Details: Full name, official email address, mobile phone number, login password (stored exclusively as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash), registration category (Delegate, Academic/Student, Exhibitor, Sponsor), organization or institution name, designation, city, student identification number (for student delegates), and preferred attendance schedule days.
  • Passport Details (Foreign Nationals only): Passport number, country of issuance, expiration date, and name as shown on passport. This data is collected strictly for mandatory government security clearances and international delegate venue accreditation.
  • App Usage & Preferences: Selected technology domains of interest, bookmarked conference sessions, text questions submitted to the in-app FAQ/Assistant search, and digital badge venue check-in records.
  • Microphone (Optional Voice Search): If you choose to use the in-app voice assistant/search, audio is transiently processed by your device's built-in operating system speech-recognition service (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO). Tec-Verse does NOT record, save, or store any audio recordings.
Data We Do NOT Collect: The Tec-Verse 2026 app does NOT access or collect your device GPS location, address book/contacts, camera photos, media library, or advertising identifiers (no Google Advertising ID tracking). We do not display third-party advertisements or sell user data.

2. Purpose and How We Use Your Data

  • To register attendees, issue unique encrypted digital badges (QR codes), and verify admission at the Chennai Trade Centre venue.
  • To authenticate accounts and deliver transactional one-time passwords (OTPs) for email verification and password resets.
  • To send official event notifications, schedule changes, and logistical announcements.
  • To manage venue capacity, security compliance, and session participation.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize attendee information. Access to data is restricted strictly to:

  • Authorized C-DAC administrative and event organizing personnel.
  • Authorized government cloud hosting infrastructure providers operating under strict government cybersecurity and data protection standards.
  • Government security and law enforcement agencies if strictly required under applicable Indian laws for national security and VIP event safety.
  • External Map Navigation: If you choose to get venue directions, the starting/destination location is opened directly in your device's chosen map application (Google Maps / Apple Maps).

4. Data Security and Cryptographic Protection

All data transmitted between the Tec-Verse mobile application and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS. User authentication credentials use salted cryptographic hashing. Session tokens are held inside secure local storage on the device. Access to administrative systems is restricted with multi-tier role-based access control.

5. Account Deletion and Data Retention (Google Play Requirement)

You have the absolute right to delete your account and personal data at any time:
  • In the App: Sign in → go to Profile → tap Delete Account and confirm.
  • On the Web (No App Install Required): You can submit an account deletion request through our public portal at:
    https://tec-verse.cdac.in/account-deletion

Deleting your account permanently removes your registration record, digital entry badge, saved technology interests, and notification history from all Tec-Verse production servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Tec-Verse 2026 is an advanced technology convention intended for technology professionals, university students, and researchers aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
